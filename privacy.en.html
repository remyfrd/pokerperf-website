Privacy Policy

PokerPerf app · Last updated: 24 September 2026

This English version is a courtesy translation. In the event of any discrepancy, the French version prevails and is the sole legally binding text.

This policy describes the data processed by the PokerPerf mobile app (poker training), why it is processed, where it is stored, and the rights you have. It is written to reflect the actual behaviour of the app.

The data controller is Rémy Ferrand, sole trader (the "we"). For any question about your data: contact@pokerperf.com.

The essentials
  • You can use the app with an account (email, password, optional first name).
  • Some of your data stays only on your phone; the rest is stored on our servers (hosted in the European Union).
  • We do not sell your data and do not run targeted advertising.
  • The app handles no banking data: payments are processed by Apple, Google and RevenueCat.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app.

1. The account

The app works with an account, needed to save your progression and manage the subscription. When creating the account, we process:

  • Email address — login identifier and account verification.
  • Password — transmitted to create the account, then kept on the server only in encrypted (hashed) form; we never store your password in clear text.
  • First name (optional) — to personalise the welcome.

Email verification: after signup we send a 6-digit code to your address to confirm it belongs to you. This code is sent via our email provider (see §7) and is stored on the server only in encrypted, temporary form.

3. What stays only on your phone

To work offline and avoid making you log in repeatedly, the app stores locally, in the system's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and in a local database:

  • your session token (keeps you logged in);
  • a copy of your first name and your display/accessibility settings;
  • a daily exercise counter (for the free tier cap);
  • the downloaded exercise catalogue (game content, no personal data);
  • an outbound queue of your attempts and reports created offline, pending sync to the server.

These local data disappear when you log out, delete your account, or uninstall the app.

4. Payments and subscriptions

The premium subscription is managed by the app stores: App Store (Apple) and Google Play, via the RevenueCat service. Specifically:

  • The app does not collect or store any credit card data: the payment happens entirely inside your phone's operating system.
  • To attach your subscription to your account, an internal account identifier (a number, without email or name) is transmitted to RevenueCat.
  • Cancellation is done from the subscription settings of your phone (Apple or Google). Deleting your PokerPerf account does not automatically cancel the subscription: remember to cancel it via your store.

5. Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules local notifications (for example a trial-ending reminder). They are generated directly on your phone: we do not use server push notifications and no "notification token" is sent to our servers.

6. Audience measurement

We use PostHog (hosted in the European Union) to anonymously measure app usage (app opens, home progression, exercises completed, subscription steps). These statistics:

  • contain no data allowing you to be identified (no email, no first name, no account identifier);
  • rely on an anonymous identifier regenerated at each launch of the app (no persistent device tracking);
  • are aggregated and serve only to improve the app; they are neither sold nor used for advertising.

As this is audience measurement strictly necessary to improve the service and devoid of any identifying data, it does not require your prior consent; this notice serves that purpose.

8. Retention period

  • Account and play data (server): kept as long as your account exists. There is no scheduled automatic deletion.
  • Account deletion: when you delete your account, your server data (account, play history, reports) is erased permanently and immediately, with no anonymised copy kept.
  • Local data (phone): kept until logout, account deletion or app uninstall.
  • Anonymous statistics: kept in aggregated form, with no link to your identity.

9. Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data.

Delete your account and your data

You can delete your account yourself from within the app: Settings → Delete account. This action erases your local data on the phone and triggers the permanent erasure of your data on our servers.

To exercise your other rights or for any question, write to us at contact@pokerperf.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

10. Minors

PokerPerf is a poker training app reserved for adults (18 years and older). It is not aimed at minors and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

11. The pokerperf.com website

On the pokerperf.com website, if you enter your email address in the "get notified at launch" form, it is processed for the sole purpose of sending you a notification when the app launches. The form is handled by the Formspree provider, which receives and forwards your request to us. You may at any time request the removal of your address by writing to contact@pokerperf.com.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log technical connection data (such as your IP address) in its server logs, in accordance with its own policy.

12. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or to regulations. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page; significant changes will be flagged to you inside the app.
