Who's behind it

Built by enthusiasts, for players

PokerPerf is built by a team of poker enthusiasts. It comes from a simple observation: you learn the theory, but at the table you have to decide fast and well. Hence a rare stance in poker: real accessibility (colors paired with patterns and text, keyboard and screen-reader navigation) and careful craft — because a good trainer should be usable by everyone.
